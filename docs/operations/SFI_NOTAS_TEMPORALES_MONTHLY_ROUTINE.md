# SFI Notas Temporales — monthly routine

Status: implementation note.

`Notas Temporales` is the institutional monthly publication, not the name of individual essays. The monthly routine must reuse the existing World Observatory, World Vector, AMV, Predictive Engine, Method Lab and canonical publication surfaces. It must not create a parallel blog engine, prediction engine, agent swarm, evidence store or publication database.

## Operating cadence

Daily infrastructure already performs World observation, hypothesis generation/calibration, continuity, institutional-cycle work, indicator capture and predictive reconciliation. The monthly lane composes those existing traces into one bounded issue candidate for the previous calendar month.

The monthly candidate should contain, at minimum:

- observed signals and dominant World Vector deltas;
- hypotheses opened during the period, their falsification signals and current outcome state;
- hypothesis outcomes, contradictions and learning observed during the period;
- selected Method Lab investigations and explicit limitations;
- a bounded Signal Vane reading over the monthly context;
- a bounded Cluster Atlas reading over the same context;
- Predictive Engine health/calibration state and due-window observations, without converting prediction into fact;
- missingness, rival explanations and unresolved questions;
- a publication boundary separating OBSERVED, DERIVED, INFERRED, PROJECTED and MISSING material.

## Founder-away rule

The routine may collect, classify, compare, synthesize and persist a monthly publication candidate without founder approval. It must not fabricate evidence, promote inference to canon, create irreversible external action or silently redefine institutional authority. Existing continuity and governance rules remain authoritative.

## Publication boundary

A monthly routine may prepare a publication candidate automatically. Canonical publication remains governed by the existing publication/canonical-object architecture. When a recurring monthly publication path is explicitly admitted as routine institutional authority, that admission must be encoded as a bounded policy rather than implemented as an unrestricted repository writer.

## VANE / ATLAS / Predictive boundary

Signal Vane and Cluster Atlas are context-driven, non-executing AMV instruments. They may be invoked by the monthly routine against the bounded monthly context; they do not acquire their own persistence plane or external execution authority. Predictive Engine remains the persisted governed-learning subsystem and is observed/calibrated independently.
