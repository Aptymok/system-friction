# SFI Surface Contract

## Purpose

System Friction Institute is one operational system with five distinct surfaces. Each surface has a bounded responsibility. Data may move between surfaces only through persisted records and existing authenticated contracts.

## FIELD

FIELD is the place where an appearance is preserved before judgment.

It may contain:

- declared objectives;
- observations and marks;
- evidence;
- returns;
- persistent-signal candidates;
- phenomenon and phenotype candidates;
- attractors and ejectors;
- reversible minimum-perturbation proposals;
- longitudinal learning.

A single observation must not be presented as a diagnosis, phenotype or validated phenomenon.

## STUDIO

STUDIO receives an observed object or signal and supports analysis, modelling, simulation, transformation and production.

Every STUDIO operation should retain:

- origin;
- evidence and provenance;
- capability used;
- transformation performed;
- output;
- conditions for return or verification.

A STUDIO output becomes institutionally meaningful only when it can return to FIELD or ROOT as an observable result, intervention, evidence item or governed proposal.

## WORLD FIELD / MAP

The World Field localizes public events, observations and tensions.

It answers where an observation manifests. It does not replace the aggregated public Observatory and must not imply causal relations that are not persisted or calculated.

## PUBLIC OBSERVATORY

The Public Observatory publishes aggregated, longitudinal and methodologically bounded readings.

It must not expose:

- private cases;
- personal evidence;
- internal governance;
- unpublished hypotheses;
- operational credentials or payloads.

## ROOT

ROOT is the private governance and institutional observability console of SFI.

It is founder-restricted and absent from normal-user navigation. It may observe agents, evidence, memory, predictions, learning, proposals, approvals and operational warnings.

Routine observation and calibration should not require recurring founder validation. Human authorization is reserved for governed publication, external outreach, sensitive mutation and explicit closure.

## Shared epistemic sequence

All surfaces use the same sequence:

1. Observed
2. Preserved
3. Related
4. Hypothesis
5. Persistent signal candidate
6. Phenomenon candidate
7. Phenotype candidate
8. Reversible test or intervention
9. Return observed
10. Validated, contradicted, inconclusive or learned

Missing information remains missing. No surface may generate decorative nodes, fallback relations, default measurements or synthetic evidence and present them as observations.

## Canonical transitions

- FIELD to STUDIO: observed object, persistent signal, evidence or perturbation candidate.
- STUDIO to FIELD: transformed object, intervention condition, simulation result or verification request.
- FIELD and STUDIO to ROOT: evidence, outcomes, learning events, predictions and governed proposals.
- WORLD FIELD to PUBLIC OBSERVATORY: aggregated and publishable observations.
- ROOT to PUBLIC OBSERVATORY: only approved public readings.

ROOT is never a required destination for a normal user.
