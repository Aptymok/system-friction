# SFI-DT-CLAIM-BOUNDARY-1.0

Status: **FROZEN**

## Scope

`N_subject = 1`

EXP-001 may support claims about the performance of the frozen SFI-DT-1.0 instrument on one founder naturalistic target under the declared arm matrix.

## Permitted claims

Subject to valid receipts and complete protocol execution, SFI may report:

- validated structural fidelity for each frozen arm;
- the predeclared `CT_FULL - B5_RULE_STRUCTURE` contrast;
- descriptive incremental contrasts between adjacent baselines;
- whether empirical evidence was sufficient or `BLOCKED`;
- instrument failures, invalid runs, and uncertainty.

## Prohibited claims

EXP-001 does not establish:

- phenomenal consciousness;
- subjective experience;
- identity continuity in the human sense;
- sentience or personhood;
- equivalence to a human mind;
- generalization to other people;
- causal superiority of Cognitive Twin architecture in a population;
- universality of the current thresholds or structural weights.

## Epistemic rule

Evidence precedes inference. Simulation may exercise the apparatus but cannot substitute for observed validation. Missing empirical evidence remains missing; it is not converted into a favorable or unfavorable score.
