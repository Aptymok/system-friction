## Purpose

Track three distinct research programs that can share temporally bounded evidence, reconstruction methods and minimal-sufficiency questions, without conflating subjects, endpoints, temporal cutoffs or validation status:

1. **LCI — Longitudinal Individuation Protocol:** whether the particular developmental biography of an *artificial agent/CT-A subject* adds out-of-sample predictive or explanatory information when model, seed, architecture and memory controls are held constant. **Not a claim about human personal identity.**
2. **SFI-DT EXP-001 — Decision Transfer:** whether a governed Cognitive Twin reconstructs the *structure of one future naturalistic decision* more faithfully than the B5 rules/constraints baseline. It is not a claim of consciousness or human identity equivalence.
3. **MIHM-T EXP-001 — Temporal Friction:** whether explicit latent dependencies and heterogeneous operational clocks add out-of-sample value for identifying potentially aggravating interventions and reconstructing feasible system trajectories.

The proposed **PEMS — Persistencia Estructural Mínima Suficiente** is a *cross-experiment research property*, not a fourth experiment, not a new MIHM Phi, and not an empirically validated invariant.

## Unifying research question

**What minimally sufficient set of state, history, relations, time conditions, constraints and provenance must persist for a bounded subject, decision structure or system trajectory to retain a specified, empirically testable functional property across transformations?**

This is always **relative to the declared object, task, environment and time horizon**. Preservation of a prediction, audit property, semantic hash or lineage does not by itself establish persistence of a conscious self or physical/material identity.

## PEMS — operational candidate

Let `H` be the eligible historical information set; `S ⊆ H` a reduced, provenance-preserving representation; `L_task(S)` a task-specific out-of-sample loss; `K` a preregistered set of non-negotiable invariants.

Seek a **candidate** `S*` minimizing representation/observation cost subject to:

- `L_task(S) <= L_task(H) + delta_task`, where `delta_task` is a *predeclared*, domain-specific noninferiority tolerance, not a universal percentage;
- `I_k(S)=true` for every indispensable `k∈K`;
- when individuation is claimed operationally, `specificity(S) > specificity(matched-other-history)` by a preregistered criterion;
- timing, admissibility and provenance survive reconstruction; unknown historical facts remain unknown.

A smaller observed subset is **not** proven globally minimal unless the search algorithm and assumptions establish global optimality. A high aggregate percentage cannot compensate for a failed hard invariant (e.g. wrong authorizer of an executed action).

### Track-specific losses and invariants

| Track | Example primary evaluation | Invariants that must not silently fail |
|---|---|---|
| LCI | Biography predictive advantage, cross-lineage specificity, reconstruction accuracy on sealed holdouts | Developmental-lineage reference, correct held-out boundary, model/seed controls, no unauthorized authority expansion. |
| SFI-DT | Frozen `validated_structural_fidelity` and `CT_FULL - B5_RULE_STRUCTURE` contrast | Seven frozen arms, weights, subject `N=1`, pre-reveal target, time-admissible evidence and target commitment. |
| MIHM-T | Predeclared comparative error for an intervention-aggravation endpoint (still candidate) | Chronology, event/knowledge/execution distinction, rival hypotheses, intervention conditions and real RETURN. |

**Percent-difference rule:** do not choose a global allowed difference (e.g. 5%). Specify units, costs of false positives/negatives, confidence intervals, practical margin and study scope for each endpoint before evaluating any reserved case. Hard authority or provenance violations are not averaged into statistical tolerance. The pre-existing MIHM Phi-family metrics cannot be averaged across methods.

## Shared historical-case workflow (exploration and calibration only)

1. **Case eligibility and boundary:** real source IDs, admissible record versions, responsible actors, obligations, system states, decisions, interventions and observed return where available.
2. **Temporal ledger:** distinguish `occurred_at`, `recorded_at`, `known_at`, authority-at-time, action execution and independently observed return. Do not infer availability from event time.
3. **Truthful reconstruction:** state what can be recovered from records; label explanatory bridges as inference, not actual past agent outputs.
4. **Reverse explanatory candidates:** generate rival conditions and trajectories; identify which prior signals were genuinely knowable before cutoff.
5. **Bounded ablations:** remove redundant items and selected relations while preserving frozen inputs and provenance; measure loss, specificity and violation of hard invariants.
6. **Holdout testing:** freeze models, exact information and hypotheses prior to reveal; evaluate on independent, previously unused cases and prospective real decisions.
7. **RETURN and learning:** record supported, contradicted and unresolved claims. Learning may propose candidate updates but cannot automatically mutate canonical memory, authority or validated experimental outcomes.

Historical dossiers may be useful across programs but **sharing a dossier is not sharing an independent test**. Records already known to the model, the researcher or the training corpus cannot be truthfully advertised as blind or prospective.

## Agent event reconstruction — minimum accountable chain

To answer **"what did the agent infer; what was proposed; who authorized it; what was executed; what actually happened?"** preserve where recorded:

`SOURCE/OBSERVATION → EVIDENCE → INFERENCE (logged output only) → PROPOSAL/DECISION → AUTHORITY (actor, scope, effective interval) → EXECUTION (tool receipt) → RETURN (independent outcome) → CONTRAST`.

Never conflate a proposal with authorization, authorization with execution, execution with an external result, or unlogged hidden model reasoning with a recoverable historical inference.

An auditable case should report **what is missing** and stop before claiming completeness. Absence of a record is not evidence the event did not occur.

## Research separations and contamination barriers

- **LCI subject:** artificial CT-A lineages; human biography cannot silently become CT institutional state.
- **DT subject:** bounded person and real naturalistic choice; retrospective case studies can calibrate procedures, but cannot satisfy the frozen future-target rule.
- **MIHM-T subject:** bounded system trajectories and interventions; a known failure cannot yield prospective prediction proof.
- Shared case and snapshot identifiers require case-level permission and ethical/privacy checks, but **do not imply cross-experiment evidence admissibility**.
- Cross-study re-use of data must be logged as dependent evidence. No shared success score or collective "validation" label.
- Maintain frozen contracts and their predeclared time cutoffs. Exploratory PEMS ablations cannot rewrite SFI-DT's locked arms/weights or LCI's JSON preregistration.
